The toolkit

Seventeen building blocks, ready to become WordPress blocks

Every story here is put together from the same parts. Each part would become a block the team can drop into a post, with the words and pictures of the next story.

  1. 01

    The guide

    One object travels the whole story and lands on things: Ana’s phone, the plane over Madre de Dios, a paper boat.

    Seen inThe OfferThe Forest LineUpstream

  2. 02

    Sticky scenes

    An illustration holds still while the text scrolls over it, and the camera moves to what each line is about.

    Seen inThe OfferA Bowl of Soup

  3. 03

    The route map

    A route that draws itself as you scroll, with a distance bar and stops that light up.

    Seen inThe Offer

  4. 04

    The fly-over

    One long picture scrolls under the guide, with a zone bar, like the Reuters Kill Zone flight.

    Seen inThe Forest Line

  5. 05

    Close-up circles

    Zoomed-in details that pop out of the landscape as you pass them.

    Seen inThe Forest LineA Bowl of Soup

  6. 06

    The cut-away

    A building or a tree opened up, with rings that point at what matters.

    Seen inThe OfferThe Forest Line

  7. 07

    Counters

    Numbers that add up as you read: in The Offer, a debt that can never be paid off.

    Seen inThe Offer

  8. 08

    Spot the signs

    Numbered markers on a scene, one sign per step, so readers learn what to look for.

    Seen inThe Offer

  9. 09

    The loop

    A diagram a dot travels around, step by step: from forest to bottle and back.

    Seen inThe Forest Line

  10. 10

    The year counter

    A date that counts as the story moves through time, over a real map.

    Seen inA Bowl of SoupSlavery Changes Shape

  11. 11

    One square, one person

    A unit chart that colors itself in, group by group, as you read.

    Seen inA Bowl of SoupSlavery Changes Shape

  12. 12

    Then and now

    Two pictures of the same place; scrolling wipes from one to the other.

    Seen inA Bowl of Soup

  13. 13

    The thread

    A line that draws itself down the page, linking the eras of a long history.

    Seen inSlavery Changes Shape

  14. 14

    The paper trail

    Things that pile up as you read: here, a century of laws and treaties.

    Seen inSlavery Changes Shape

  15. 15

    The panorama

    One wide picture that pans sideways as you scroll down: a river walked upstream.

    Seen inUpstream

  16. 16

    The glossary

    A dictionary page that turns with each card, one word at a time.

    Seen inUpstream

  17. 17

    The time-lapse

    One illustrated place that changes with each card: the coast, the ponds, the storm.

    Seen inWhen the Land Breaks
