What The Offer is built from

Six building blocks, ready to become WordPress blocks

  1. The guideOne object that travels the whole story and lands on things: Ana’s phone, like the Reuters drone.
  2. Sticky scenesAn illustration holds still while the text scrolls over it, and the camera moves to what each line is about.
  3. The fly-over mapA route that draws itself as you scroll, with a distance bar and stops that light up.
  4. The cut-awayA building opened up, floor by floor, with rings that point at what matters.
  5. CountersNumbers that add up as you read: here, a debt that can never be paid off.
  6. Spot the signsNumbered markers on a scene, one sign per step, so readers learn what to look for.
