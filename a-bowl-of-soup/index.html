    A Bowl of Soup

    How a soup program in an Amsterdam safe house grew into Dignita, a restaurant that trains survivors of trafficking for real jobs.

    About 6 minutes · October 2026

    The kitchens are illustrated and no real person is shown. The facts and figures come from Not For Sale’s work in the Netherlands.

    2012, Amsterdam

    Not For Sale’s work in the Netherlands began in the Red Light District.

    There, social worker Toos Heemskerk-Schep and Not For Sale started Home Soup, in the kitchen of a safe house.

    Survivors made soup and sold it. They came away with a certificate, a skill and an income.

    Survivors of trafficking do not need saving. They need opportunity.

    2015

    The soup becomes a restaurant. Dignita opens in the Vondelpark: a brunch place that has to win its guests like any other.

    2017

    A second Dignita opens in the Hoftuin, a garden in the east of the city center.

    2020

    A third, in Westerpark.

    Today

    Dignita runs from the Hoftuin, on the Nieuwe Herengracht, open every day. The Vondelpark and Westerpark restaurants have closed.

    What made it work? Dignita is a real restaurant. It has to win its guests like any other brunch place.

    Its Academy, built with ROC van Amsterdam, gives survivors government-accredited training on the job.

    Trainees choose a track: the kitchen, the coffee bar, nail styling and beauty, or administration through FAIR Finances, set up in 2021.

    What they make is served to paying guests from the first day.

    And every euro of profit goes back into training the next group.

    About this story

    Opportunity, not rescue

    Dignita is not a charity kitchen. It is a restaurant that has to earn its guests, and that is the point: trainees learn inside a real business, and the business pays for their training. All profits return to Stichting Dignita to fund the next group of trainees.

    Visit Dignita Hoftuin at Nieuwe Herengracht 18a, Amsterdam. It is open every day from 09:30 to 16:30, as listed on Not For Sale’s site.
